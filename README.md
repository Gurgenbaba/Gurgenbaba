## 🛰 CURRENTLY BUILDING


### 🌌 Genesis Colonies
Founder & Developer


A long-term game project built around exploration, progression,
expeditions and a persistent galaxy.


Building the systems behind the universe — not just the surface.


---


### ◉ Life OS
Founder & Developer


A personal operating system for everyday life.


Life OS is being built to connect things that are normally scattered
across different apps:


`Documents` · `Tasks` · `Deadlines` · `Contracts` · `Calendar` · `Life Objects`


The goal isn't to replace every tool people already use.


**Connect, don't replace.**


Life OS is designed to become the coordination layer between your
real life and the digital services you already depend on.


Currently building the foundation across:


`iOS` · `Android` · `Web` · `API` · `PostgreSQL` · `Redis`


---

## 🧭 WHAT I'M INTERESTED IN


I like building systems where a lot of moving parts eventually
become something simple to use.


Right now that means two very different problems:


**Genesis Colonies**
→ building a persistent game universe and the systems behind it.


**Life OS**
→ figuring out how software can make everyday organization less fragmented.


Different products.


Same obsession with building the underlying system properly.
